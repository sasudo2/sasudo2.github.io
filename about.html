About

Building useful ML under real constraints

I am a Computer Engineering student at IOE Thapathali, based in Kathmandu. I focus on applied machine learning where data is incomplete, problem definitions are fuzzy, and resources are constrained.

I care less about polished demos and more about whether a model can survive messy conditions and still deliver measurable value.

How I Work

  • Just me: I can scope, collect or label data, train, evaluate, and write up a complete case study end-to-end.
  • Delegate: I can define clear specs for repetitive labeling, runbooks for experiments, and handoff-ready documentation.
  • Collaborate: I communicate tradeoffs with product, engineering, and founders when technical choices affect timeline, quality, or cost.
